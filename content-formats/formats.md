# Video Project Formats

This file defines the available video project categories and their default creative constraints. Defaults can be overridden by the user. Add future categories here without expanding the `content-formats` entry skill.

## TikTok Product Promotion

**Purpose:** Make a product immediately understandable and visually compelling in a short vertical social video, with a clear reason for the intended viewer to care.

### Default constraints

- Vertical 9:16. In the local MiniMax H3 app, dimensions must be divisible by 32 (for example, 768×1344).
- Maximum runtime: 15 seconds, including an end card.
- The product is the visual and narrative focus. Show its recognizable form clearly and include a meaningful use moment.
- Identify likely use context and intended audience before choosing presenter, location, wardrobe, and tone.
- Presenters and realistic human characters should be adults with a natural, contemporary appearance appropriate to the target market; by default use people who read as living outside China, as requested for this format. Avoid caricature or assigning a nationality without evidence or need.
- Default delivery is direct-to-camera presenter speech, supported by demonstrations and product close-ups. Use concise conversational English unless another market or language is specified.
- Open with a visual or spoken hook in the first seconds; establish a relatable need or routine, show product use, then close with a benefit-led takeaway or call to action.
- Keep one main message and a few visual beats. Avoid dense exposition or elaborate subplot.
- Describe only substantiated product attributes. Do not invent clinical results, guarantees, before/after transformations, safety claims, ingredient concentrations, or endorsements. For skincare/body-care, avoid implying medical treatment or guaranteed outcomes.
- Preserve phone-screen readability and caption safe zones. Do not rely on generated readable package text; use a product reference image or add text in editing.

### Script handoff

Include working title, audience/use context, core message, hook, timed beats totaling no more than 15 seconds, spoken lines, visible product action, tone, and factual/claims boundaries. A short ad may be a beat sheet rather than a full dramatic outline; screenplay-development should adapt its depth to the form.

### Shot-planning handoff

Keep vertical composition and product readability explicit in every shot. Plan a small achievable set of shots, prioritize presenter and product interaction, and specify clean transitions and caption-safe area. The shot breakdown still follows the canonical concepts and shot JSON schema.

## Animated Narrative Film

**Purpose:** Tell a character-driven animated story with an intentional emotional journey rather than a product-led social ad.

### Default constraints

- Characters are designed as 2D or 3D animation; specify medium and visual approach when known.
- Establish protagonist, desire, obstacle, stakes, and meaningful relationships.
- Shape a legible beginning, escalation, climax, and ending. Build emotional rises and falls, with a climax caused by character choices and an ending that shows consequences.
- Runtime and aspect ratio are project-specific unless the user sets them.
- Use screenplay-development to iterate premise, character dynamics, causal plot, and ending. Then use shot-planning to translate the approved story into visual coverage.
